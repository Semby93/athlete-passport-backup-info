Use conditions

Athlete Passport Backups is an administrative recovery tool operated by Jordan Sembel.

Authorized use

Use is limited to the application operator and people explicitly authorized to manage its backups. Connect only an account and data that you are authorized to use for this purpose. This site does not offer public registration, paid subscriptions or customer access to the backup tool.

Operating limits

The tool is being validated. Automatic backups and retention are not active. A successful connection or file-transfer test does not establish that an entire application can be restored. Recovery targets remain planning targets until the full workflow has been tested. Maintain existing recovery copies while validation is incomplete.

Account control

The connected account owner can revoke Google access and manage their Drive files. Deleting recovery copies or losing the separate recovery key can prevent restoration. Changes to provider availability, quotas or authorization can interrupt backups.

Privacy and contact

The privacy notice describes the Google data used by this tool. For questions or to end access, contact jordan.sembel@hotmail.com.
