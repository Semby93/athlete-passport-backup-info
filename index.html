Athlete Passport Backups helps the application operator store encrypted recovery copies in their own Google Drive and check that those copies can be downloaded.

What the Google connection does

The operator signs in with Google and authorizes access to files created by this tool or explicitly selected for it. The connection supports uploading encrypted backups, reading them back for verification, and managing approved backup-copy retention.

This tool is for the operator's administrative use. Athletes, coaches and organizations do not need to connect their Google accounts to it.

Current status

The connection and a synthetic upload/download have been tested. Automatic backups and automatic retention are not enabled. The planned schedule is a backup attempt every six hours with 30-day backup-copy retention, subject to operational validation.
